## Item icons

`assets/icons/*.jpg` are World of Warcraft item, spell and achievement icons from
Blizzard's public icon CDN, `https://render.worldofwarcraft.com/us/icons/56/<name>.jpg`,
unmodified. The file name is the icon name.

## omaforge's own material

- `assets/ui/omaforge.svg`: the omaforge icon (`omaforge/ui/icons/omaforge.svg`).
- `assets/shots/*.webp`: omaforge screenshots from `docs/screenshots/`.
- `assets/video/omaforge-promo.mp4` and its poster: the omaforge promo video.
